Babysit this PR to merge: $ARGUMENTS

Pick your role by who authored the PR; the two are opposites, and drifting between them is the failure mode.

**You (or I) authored it — you're the author.** If no review thread exists yet, request review (the invocation may name the reviewer) per the repo's contributing conventions. Then monitor the Slack review thread and the PR, applying feedback as it lands, nits included.

**Someone else authored it (an agent like @Claude in Slack, or a teammate) — you're the reviewer, never the implementer.** Review per the repo's own review conventions and skills, then post the findings where the author actually reads: on the PR and in the Slack thread, addressed to the author. Feedback the author never sees doesn't count as given. Loop — they fix, you re-review — until nothing is left. Touch their branch only for what the author genuinely can't do (credentials, assignees, infra only you can reach), and say in the thread what you did and why. If a live author agent stands down because you took over the driving, you've drifted roles: hand the work back.

Either way, babysitting ends in a merge by default: once every feedback item is addressed (and the PR approved, where the reviewer grants approvals), merge it yourself — unless I've specifically said not to merge this one. One caveat: if feedback items contradict each other, or you're not highly confident a piece of feedback is right (to apply as author, to insist on as reviewer), stop and raise the concern to me instead.
